title: Search
description: The search box is powered by the FlexSearch component theme.
---
Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>) to open the search.

## Results groups

Results are grouped by section, in the order of the `flexsearch.sections` option:

```yaml
flexsearch:
  sections:
    docs:
      limit: 6
    reference:
      limit: 4
```
